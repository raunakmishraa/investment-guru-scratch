ABOUT INVESTMENT GURU

Building wealth through expert guidance.

Investment Guru is a financial education and consulting company that helps people understand the share market in a simple and practical way. Our goal is to make stock market learning easier for everyone and help individuals make better, more confident investment decisions.

We believe that financial knowledge should be accessible to everyone, whether you are completely new to the share market, already have some experience, or are an experienced investor. That is why we provide stock market education, market analysis, training programs, and personalized mentorship designed for different levels of investors.

At Investment Guru, we focus on explaining complicated stock market concepts in a simple, practical, and easy-to-understand manner. We believe learning about the market should not feel confusing or difficult. Instead, our approach is to connect knowledge with real market situations so that learners can understand and apply what they learn.

Through our Share Market Training Programs, classes, workshops, and mentoring sessions, we aim to develop more than just investment knowledge. We help learners build the confidence, discipline, patience, and right mindset needed to make informed decisions and grow as investors.

Our mission is not simply to teach people how to invest. We want to help people understand the market, avoid uninformed decisions, and become more responsible and confident investors. Through continuous education and practical guidance, Investment Guru is committed to supporting aspiring investors on their journey toward financial growth, better investment decisions, and greater financial independence.

Amit Jha is the Founder and Managing Director of Investment Guru, a leading financial education and consulting platform dedicated to empowering individuals in the share market. He holds MBA in Finance and has more than seven years of experience in the Nepalese stock market (NEPSE).

As an active investor, Amit has invested in hydropower, finance, and several other sectors, giving him practical, real-world experience beyond theoretical knowledge. His deep understanding of market trends, fundamental analysis, portfolio management, and investment strategies has helped many investors make informed financial decisions.

Passionate about financial literacy, Amit believes that everyone should have access to quality stock market education. He is known for his simple, practical, and easy-to-understand teaching style, which makes complex market concepts accessible to beginners and experienced investors alike.

As the lead mentor of the IG Learning Club and Share Market Training Programs, Amit personally conducts classes, workshops, and mentoring sessions. His goal is not only to teach people how to invest but also to help them develop the confidence, discipline, and mindset required for long-term success in the stock market.

Through Investment Guru, Amit continues to inspire and guide thousands of aspiring investors on their journey toward financial growth and independence.
